Single-Channel 20W Solid-State Guitar Amplifier

Maciej Rogoż
4 Jan 2018
analog-electronics, audio, amplifier, hardware

A compact 20W single-channel solid-state guitar amplifier engineered for clean audio amplification and active tone shaping.

Project Overview

A standalone analog audio amplifier featuring a preamplifier and active EQ controls, housed in a custom wooden enclosure.

Key Features

  • Power Output: 20W solid-state power amplifier block.
  • Control Interface: Dedicated Volume, Treble, Bass, and Gain controls.
  • Role: Circuit Design, Assembly & Enclosure Wiring
