Hi! I’m Maciej, a 4th-year Microelectronics in Technology and Medicine student at AGH University of Krakow.

I focus on analog and RF circuit design, PCB layout, and hardware testing. Currently, I am working on my engineering thesis regarding a 24 GHz Low Noise Amplifier (LNA) for radar applications.

Technical Expertise

  • EDA & PCB Design: Cadence Virtuoso, Altium Designer, KiCad, LTspice
  • Digital Systems & FPGA: SystemVerilog, Vivado, Quartus, Logisim
  • Programming & Tools: C, Python (DSP), LabVIEW, Linux, Git

Student Research & Leadership

As an active member of the AGH Logic Unit research group, I am responsible for schematic design and PCB layout.

Beyond Engineering

When I’m not in the lab or simulation tools:

  • Audio Electronics & Luthiery: I design and build custom guitar effects, as well as craft traditional stringed instruments like lutes and Byzantine lyres.
  • Music: Active guitarist and multi-instrumentalist in a band.
  • 3D Printing: Designing models for practical enclosures and mechanical prototypes.
  • Sports: Former competitive rower — Polish National Champion (2017).
